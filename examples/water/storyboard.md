# Water cycle — guided storyboard

Learning goal: after the lesson, the viewer should understand that the water cycle is a connected system spanning atmosphere, surface water, terrain, rivers, soil and groundwater.

## Step 1 — Evaporation · 0–5s

**Narration:** Solar energy warms the ocean. Some surface water becomes vapor and enters the atmosphere.

**Visible change:** Sunlight strengthens; vapor particles rise from the ocean surface.

**Takeaway:** Evaporation transfers liquid water from the surface into the atmosphere.

## Step 2 — Vapor transport & condensation · 5–10s

**Narration:** Wind carries moisture inland. Rising air cools, so vapor condenses into cloud droplets and ice crystals.

**Visible change:** The vapor stream shifts toward land; the cloud moves toward the mountains and becomes denser.

**Takeaway:** Wind transports moisture, while uplift and cooling create clouds.

## Step 3 — Precipitation · 10–15s

**Narration:** Cloud droplets collide and grow until gravity overcomes the upward air motion.

**Visible change:** The cloud darkens slightly and rain particles fall toward the mountain, valley and soil.

**Takeaway:** Precipitation returns atmospheric water to the surface.

## Step 4 — Surface runoff · 15–20s

**Narration:** Water that does not immediately infiltrate follows terrain downhill, enters streams and rivers, and eventually returns to the ocean.

**Visible change:** The river tube brightens and moving water markers travel from mountain to coast.

**Takeaway:** Topography routes surface water back toward the sea.

## Step 5 — Infiltration & groundwater · 20–25s

**Narration:** Some precipitation seeps into soil and rock, recharging groundwater that moves slowly through porous layers.

**Visible change:** Vertical infiltration particles move below the surface and the underground flow path lights up toward the coast.

**Takeaway:** Groundwater is a hidden but important reservoir in the water cycle.

## Interaction

- Click any step in the left rail to jump directly to its beginning.
- Previous/next controls step through the lesson.
- Scrubbing the timeline deterministically reconstructs the same water state.
- Chinese and English are switched as complete UI languages; they are never mixed on the same screen.
